01 / Visual language

Neutral surfaces, graphite fittings, precise instrument labels and restrained teal. Opaque readable panels; clear solutions remain clear.

02 / VR prototype dimensions

0.42 m panel at about 1 m; 0.032 m menu text and 0.045 m button height are starting values. Actual headset readability and reach require testing.

03 / Functional acceptance

Actual input-driven VR and Desktop journeys, native events and inventory reconciliation. Every state and action must be wired; screenshots alone do not prove operation.
